Who we are

BTT United Company Limited designs and delivers motor starter solutions and switchgear for industry. Our focus is efficient electrical equipment: motor protection and control, power supply quality, industrial electrical protection, power factor correction and harmonic filtering.

Our engineers work with plant owners and contractors from the first site survey through commissioning and after-sales service, so every motor is started, protected and monitored the right way.
